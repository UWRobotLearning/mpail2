Your Turn

Try MPAIL2 yourself!


Making strides in real-world observational learning means that you can train your own robots in the real world without relying on pre-trained models or data that may not apply to your setup or task.

The only input to the algorithm is demonstration observations. You don't even need to record the actions!


The full code, pretrained models (as available), and instructions for reproducing our main experiments are released on GitHub. You can start by:

  • Cloning the repository from github.com/UWRobotLearning/mpail2.
  • Following the README to set up dependencies and run the provided training and evaluation scripts.
  • Adapting the tasks and observation streams to your own robot or environment.
We welcome extensions to new embodiments, sensors, and tasks—please reach out or open an issue if you build on this work.
